SEPTEMBER 2027 INTAKE

Application.

The first Sound and Music Technology cohort starts in September 2027 at Jean Monnet University.

Application details

There are two distinct admission sessions for SMT. The first one takes place from January 4, 2027 until March 31, 2027. It targets all candidates (European and extra-European) and must be carried out through the eCandidat portal. The second admission session will take place later in the year (May/June period) and only concerns European candidates. For this second admission phase, candidates must use the Mon Master portal.
